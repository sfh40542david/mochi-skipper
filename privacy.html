Mochi Skipper Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mochi Skipper handles information when you use the app.

1. Information you provide

Mochi Skipper does not require you to create a game account or sign in. We do not ask you to provide your name, address, phone number, or other personal profile information in order to play.

2. Game data stored on your device

Game progress, scores, stardust, coin balances, settings and unlocked companions are stored locally on your device. The app does not provide an online leaderboard or game-account cloud sync.

3. In-App Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment-card information. Apple may process transaction and account information according to Apple's own privacy policies.

4. Advertising and consent

Mochi Skipper may display interstitial advertisements to non-VIP users using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising data, app interaction data, and diagnostic information as permitted by your device settings, applicable law, and any consent choices presented to you.

Where required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) or related consent mechanisms to present privacy choices. You can also manage applicable advertising/privacy permissions through your device settings.

5. Analytics

Mochi Skipper does not include a separate first-party analytics account system. However, third-party advertising services may receive diagnostic or usage information as part of providing, measuring, securing, or improving advertising services.

6. Children's privacy

Mochi Skipper does not knowingly ask users to submit personal profile information through a game account. Advertising and consent behavior may vary by region, age-related settings, and applicable platform requirements.

7. Data retention

Locally stored game data remains on your device unless it is removed through normal app/device actions. Information processed by Apple, Google, or other third parties is subject to their respective retention practices and privacy policies.

8. Third-party services

The app may rely on services provided by Apple (including the App Store and StoreKit) and Google (including Google Mobile Ads and UMP). Their handling of information is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, applicable law, or third-party services change. The latest version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact

Mochi Skipper 隱私政策

生效日期：2026 年 9 月 15 日

本隱私政策說明您使用 Mochi Skipper 時，應用程式如何處理相關資訊。

1. 您主動提供的資料

Mochi Skipper 不要求建立遊戲帳號或登入。玩家無需提供姓名、地址、電話號碼或其他個人檔案資料即可遊玩。

2. 儲存在裝置上的遊戲資料

遊戲進度、分數、星塵、金幣餘額、設定及已解鎖夥伴會儲存在您的裝置本機。遊戲目前不提供線上排行榜或遊戲帳號雲端同步。

3. App 內購買

可選的 App 內購買由 Apple 透過 App Store 及 StoreKit 處理。我們不會接收或儲存您的付款卡資料。Apple 可能依其自身隱私政策處理交易及帳戶相關資訊。

4. 廣告及同意機制

Mochi Skipper 可能透過 Google Mobile Ads（AdMob）向非 VIP 玩家顯示插頁廣告。Google 及其廣告合作夥伴可能在您的裝置設定、適用法律及您所作出的同意選擇允許的範圍內，處理裝置識別碼、IP 位址、廣告資料、App 互動資料及診斷資訊等資料。

在適用地區，遊戲會使用 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）或相關同意機制向您提供隱私選擇。您亦可透過裝置設定管理適用的廣告或隱私權限。

5. 分析資料

Mochi Skipper 沒有另外建立第一方分析帳戶系統。不過，第三方廣告服務可能因提供、衡量、保障或改善廣告服務而取得診斷或使用情況資料。

6. 兒童隱私

Mochi Skipper 不會透過遊戲帳號主動要求使用者提交個人檔案資料。廣告及同意機制可能因地區、年齡相關設定及平台適用要求而有所不同。

7. 資料保留

儲存在本機的遊戲資料會留在您的裝置上，直至透過一般 App 或裝置操作移除。由 Apple、Google 或其他第三方處理的資料，則受其各自的資料保留方式及隱私政策規範。

8. 第三方服務

本 App 可能使用 Apple 提供的服務（包括 App Store 及 StoreKit），以及 Google 提供的服務（包括 Google Mobile Ads 及 UMP）。第三方如何處理資料，受其各自的條款及隱私政策規範。

9. 政策更新

當 App 功能、適用法律或第三方服務有所變更時，我們可能更新本隱私政策。最新版會刊登於本頁，並更新生效日期。
