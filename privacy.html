遊戲、支付與廣告資料

遊戲不要求帳號。分數、免費星塵、皮膚及設定保存在裝置，可能包含於 Apple 裝置備份。沒有遊戲雲端同步。

金幣購買由 Apple App Store 處理；App 驗證交易後在本機儲存交易識別碼、餘額及金幣解鎖的皮膚。App 不讀取你的信用卡號。金幣為消耗型商品，此版本沒有跨裝置同步或重裝恢復。

本版整合 Google AdMob 插頁廣告。非 VIP 玩家每三次死亡後於局間自動播放插頁廣告；VIP 免廣告。播放不發獎勵，關閉時間由 SDK 控制。Google 及其廣告買方可能處理 IP 位址、裝置與其他識別資訊、廣告互動及診斷資料，以投放、衡量廣告及防止詐騙。實際處理依同意狀態、平台設定與適用地區而定。

適用時由 Google UMP 顯示隱私選擇。可從設定中的「廣告隱私選項」重新選擇。本版不要求跨 App 追蹤授權；這不代表所有廣告相關資料都不被處理。Google 隱私政策；Apple 隱私政策。

分享、外部連結及相片儲存

只有當你主動選擇分享時，App 才會把成績圖片及文字交給 iOS 分享介面。內容包括當局分數、角色、完美次數、連擊及路線挑戰碼，不包含姓名或帳號。由你選擇收件服務及對象；對方服務適用自己的隱私政策。

若你選擇把圖片儲存到相片，iOS 可能要求新增相片的權限；App 不讀取你的相簿。開啟支援／政策網頁時，網站主機可能依其實際設定處理一般網路請求紀錄；發行者應確保本頁描述符合所選主機的實際政策。

刪除資料與聯絡

在遊戲設定中選「清除遊戲紀錄」並確認，可清除免費遊戲進度與設定，保留已購金幣與金幣解鎖的皮膚。移除 App 也會移除其本機資料；Apple 備份的管理請使用你的裝置／iCloud 設定。此遊戲沒有需要另外刪除的使用者帳號。

如你主動寄送支援電郵，發行者會收到你提供的電郵地址、內容及附件，僅用來處理你的查詢。請不要在郵件中寄送密碼、付款資料或不必要的敏感內容。關於查詢資料的處理或刪除，可聯絡上方郵箱。

Local progress, purchases and rewarded ads

No game account is required. Progress stays on the device and may be included in Apple backups. Apple processes coin payments; the app stores verified transaction IDs, coins and coin-unlocked skins locally. The app does not receive your card number. Consumable coins have no cross-device or reinstall recovery in this version.

Google AdMob provides automatic interstitials between rounds after every three non-VIP deaths. VIP has no ads. Ads grant no reward; closing controls are supplied by the SDK. Google and advertising buyers may process IP addresses, device and other identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Data practices depend on consent, configuration and territory. UMP privacy choices are offered where applicable and can be revisited in Settings. The app does not request cross-app tracking authorization; other ad data processing may still occur.

Sharing and support

Sharing is initiated by the player and handled by the iOS share sheet. A card contains a score, companion, perfect count, combo and route code, with no name or account. The receiving service applies its own privacy policy. Saving a card to Photos may require add-only permission; the app does not read the photo library. Support messages you send voluntarily contain the information you choose to provide and are used to answer your request.

Deletion and changes

Erase Game Data clears free progress and keeps paid coins and coin-unlocked skins. Uninstalling removes local data, including the local paid wallet. Manage Apple backups through your own device settings. There is no online account to delete. If future versions change data practices, this policy and the relevant store disclosures must be updated before that version is released.
