About the game

Mochi Skipper is a one-handed jumping game for iPhone. Aim, hold to charge, release to leap across floating islands, collect power-ups, unlock companions and challenge daily routes.

Purchases and VIP

The game is free to download and offers optional in-app purchases. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. A successful eligible purchase may unlock VIP/ad-free benefits according to the offer shown in the app.

Restore purchases

If an eligible non-consumable entitlement such as VIP needs to be restored, use the Restore Purchases option provided in the app where available. Consumable coin amounts are not re-granted by a restore operation.

Offline play

Core gameplay can be played offline. Internet access is required for purchases, purchase verification/restore functions when applicable, consent messages, and advertisements.

Local game data

Progress, coin balances, settings and unlocked companions are primarily stored on the device. There is currently no game-account cloud sync or online leaderboard.

When contacting support

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, Mochi Skipper app version, and a short description of the issue. Screenshots are helpful when relevant. Please do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, or other sensitive information.

關於遊戲

Mochi Skipper 是一款單手操作的 iPhone 跳躍遊戲。選擇角度、按住蓄力、放手起跳，跨越漂浮星島，收集道具、解鎖夥伴並挑戰每日星路。

內購與 VIP

遊戲可免費下載，並提供可選的 App 內購買。付款由 Apple 透過 App Store 處理。符合條件的成功購買會依 App 內顯示的方案解鎖相應 VIP／免廣告權益。

恢復購買

如需恢復符合條件的非消耗型權益（例如 VIP），可在 App 提供相關功能時使用「恢復購買」。消耗型金幣數量不會因恢復購買而再次發放。

離線遊玩

主要玩法可離線進行。購買、適用的交易驗證／恢復功能、隱私同意訊息及廣告需要網路連線。

本機遊戲資料

進度、金幣餘額、設定及已解鎖夥伴主要儲存在裝置本機。目前沒有遊戲帳號雲端同步或線上排行榜。
